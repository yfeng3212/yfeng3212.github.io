About

I work on machine learning for clinical prediction and medical imaging. I am interested in how the way data are collected and selected shapes what a model learns, and what that means when a model is used somewhere new.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

Clinical trials in opioid use disorder

Pathology foundation models

Synthetic training data

How filtering training data written by large language models changes what a model learns.
